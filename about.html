About Us

From our launch in 2018,we have been trailing behind major search engines.We hoped to changed the dynamics of the search-engine market with the release of INGENUITY,a "decision engine" designed to display more retrieved information in search pages than was typical,thus enabling better informed decisions concerning links to follow or displaying enough information to satisfy the original query.Ingenuity also displayed related searches and the user's previous searches on the left side of the page.
